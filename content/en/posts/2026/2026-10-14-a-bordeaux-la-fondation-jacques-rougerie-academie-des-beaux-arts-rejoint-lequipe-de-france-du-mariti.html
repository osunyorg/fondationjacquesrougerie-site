On October 14, 2026, on the occasion of the 3rd edition of Forces Vives in Bordeaux, the Jacques Rougerie Foundation - Académie des beaux-arts had the honor of joining the French maritime team to present its challenges before more than 1,500 committed industry leaders.
It was represented by Amine Lehna, its Director of Development and International Relations. He spoke alongside Nathalie Mercier-Perrin, President of the Cluster Maritime Français, Jean-Charles Orsucci, President of the National Association of Elected Officials of the Coastlines (ANEL) and Jean-Chrysostome Dumont of Escale Royale.
Amine Lehna carried the Foundation's values, as it is engaged in advocacy actions focused on water-related issues: protecting oceans and coastlines, and preserving and enhancing water resources.
The Foundation thanks Nathalie Mercier-Perrin for her trust and this wonderful collective momentum, as well as Patrice Bégay and Thierry Pariente for bringing together the region's stakeholders at an event marked by exchange and sharing.

On October 14, 2026, on the occasion of the 3rd edition of Forces Vives in Bordeaux, the Jacques Rougerie Foundation - Académie des beaux-arts had the honor of joining the French maritime team to present its challenges before more than 1,500 committed industry leaders.

It was represented by Amine Lehna, its Director of Development and International Relations. He spoke alongside Nathalie Mercier-Perrin, President of the Cluster Maritime Français, Jean-Charles Orsucci, President of the National Association of Elected Officials of the Coastlines (ANEL) and Jean-Chrysostome Dumont of Escale Royale.

Amine Lehna carried the Foundation's values, as it is engaged in advocacy actions focused on water-related issues: protecting oceans and coastlines, and preserving and enhancing water resources.

The Foundation thanks Nathalie Mercier-Perrin for her trust and this wonderful collective momentum, as well as Patrice Bégay and Thierry Pariente for bringing together the region's stakeholders at an event marked by exchange and sharing.
