Under the impetus of Maram Abbas, MENA Regional Coordinator of the Junior Ambassadors, and supported by the entire regional team, the Foundation launched the second edition of its International Training Camp at the end of August. Held in several countries of the region, including Turkey, Lebanon, Tunisia, and the United Arab Emirates, this training program brought together students, architects, and experts around the challenges of architecture in the face of population displacement, rising waters, and extreme climate events. Through lectures and workshops devoted in particular to artificial intelligence, biomimicry, and architecture in extreme environments, this initiative pursues the Foundation's ambition: to train and connect a new generation of architects committed to the living environments of tomorrow

From August 28 to 31, our MENA Regional Coordinator and founder of the event, Maram Abbas with the Junior Ambassadors of the Jacques Rougerie Foundation - Académie des beaux-arts, brought together a new generation of architects, students, researchers, and innovators for their annual international architecture summer camp.

This new edition embodies exactly what the Foundation strives to encourage around the world: young people who take the initiative, build bridges across borders, and imagine new ways of inhabiting our planet.

The program opened with an inspiring speech by Jacques Rougerie, architect and founding president of the Jacques Rougerie Foundation - Académie des beaux-arts, full of encouragement to build a sustainable future.

These lectures brought together more than 700 participants, in person from Lebanon, Tunisia, Turkey, the United Arab Emirates, and Mauritius, as well as online from around the world. The discussions will now continue for a month, giving participants time to deepen their thinking and develop concrete projects.

This program was organized in collaboration with an exceptional network of universities, professional organizations, and international partners, including FIABCI, the Rebuild Green program, the Orders of Engineers and Architects in Beirut and Tripoli, Canadian University Dubai, Heriot-Watt University, American University in Dubai, Lebanese University, University of Balamand / ALBA, NDU, Ecole Nationale d'Architecture et d'Urbanisme (ENAU), Deep Blue Foundation, University of Mauritius, the Beirut Urban Planning Observatory, and the Başakşehir Innovation Laboratory, East International University, City University Lebanon, Line Group.

This initiative once again demonstrated the strength of our Junior Ambassadors network: a global community capable not only of representing the Foundation, but also of creating programs, mobilizing institutions, and connecting young talents across continents.

A huge bravo to Maram Abbas and to all the MENA Junior Ambassadors
