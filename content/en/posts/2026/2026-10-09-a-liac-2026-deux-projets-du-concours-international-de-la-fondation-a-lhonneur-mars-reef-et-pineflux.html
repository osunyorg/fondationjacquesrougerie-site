An Assistant Professor of Architecture at Beirut Arab University, he presented two scientific papers stemming from projects honored by the Foundation's International Competition: MARS REEF (2022 Grand Prize) and PineFlux (2025 Special Prize). Projects honored by the International Architecture and Innovation Competition of the Jacques Rougerie Foundation - Académie des beaux-arts continue to grow well after the awards ceremony.
From October 5 to 9, 2026, Dr. Samer El Sayary, Junior Ambassador of the Foundation, demonstrated this at IAC 2026 in Antalya, one of the largest gatherings of the space sector, bringing together NASA, ESA, and JAXA, as well as researchers, universities, and industry players from around the world.
An Assistant Professor of Architecture at Beirut Arab University, Samer presented two scientific papers stemming from projects already awarded by the Competition:
MARS REEF, Grand Prize "Architecture and Innovation for Space" in 2022, and PineFlux, Special Prize "Laboratory Architecture and Innovation" in 2025. The work on PineFlux, presented as part of the PromoMoon initiative on lunar construction technologies, received the IAF Best Interactive Presentation Award in the "Science and Exploration" category. The jury particularly praised its high scientific quality. This journey illustrates the Foundation's mission: to bring forth the pioneers of tomorrow's architecture and the future experts of the space field. The Foundation warmly congratulates Samer El Sayary on this well-deserved recognition.

Projects honored by the International Architecture and Innovation Competition of the Jacques Rougerie Foundation - Académie des beaux-arts continue to grow well after the awards ceremony.

From October 5 to 9, 2026, Dr. Samer El Sayary, Junior Ambassador of the Foundation, demonstrated this at IAC 2026 in Antalya, one of the largest gatherings of the space sector, bringing together NASA, ESA, and JAXA, as well as researchers, universities, and industry players from around the world.

An Assistant Professor of Architecture at Beirut Arab University, Samer presented two scientific papers stemming from projects already awarded by the Competition: MARS REEF, Grand Prize "Architecture and Innovation for Space" in 2022, and PineFlux, Special Prize "Laboratory Architecture and Innovation" in 2025.

The work on PineFlux, presented as part of the PromoMoon initiative on lunar construction technologies, received the IAF Best Interactive Presentation Award in the "Science and Exploration" category. The jury particularly praised its high scientific quality.

This journey illustrates the Foundation's mission: to bring forth the pioneers of tomorrow's architecture and the future experts of the space field. The Foundation warmly congratulates Samer El Sayary on this well-deserved recognition.
