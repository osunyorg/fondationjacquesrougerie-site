body: >-

After jointly supporting the first Pan-African Architecture Biennial, Etex and the

Fondation Jacques Rougerie - Académie des beaux-arts deviennent aujourd'hui partenaires.

are becoming partners today. The Foundation is proud to formalize this commitment at the Académie des beaux-arts,

at the

Palais de l'Institut de France.

In Nairobi, the Foundation and Etex jointly designed an exhibition dedicated to the projects of African Junior Ambassadors. It introduced the public to the visions of a young generation of architects and creators from the continent. Climate, water, adaptation of territories: these young talents presented concrete responses to the major challenges of our time. Their perspective fully aligns with the mission carried by the Foundation since its creation.

Strengthened by this collective success, the collaboration takes on a new dimension today. Through this partnership, the Foundation and Etex affirm a shared ambition: to support innovative, responsible architecture oriented towards the living environments of the future. This partnership opens the way to new joint initiatives in service of young architects, creators and innovators from around the world.

After jointly supporting the first Pan-African Architecture Biennial, Etex and the Fondation Jacques Rougerie - Académie des beaux-arts deviennent aujourd'hui partenaires. are becoming partners today. The Foundation is proud to formalize this commitment at the Académie des beaux-arts, at the Palais de l'Institut de France.

In Nairobi, the Foundation and Etex jointly designed an exhibition dedicated to the projects of African Junior Ambassadors. It introduced the public to the visions of a young generation of architects and creators from the continent. Climate, water, adaptation of territories: these young talents presented concrete responses to the major challenges of our time. Their perspective fully aligns with the mission carried by the Foundation since its creation.

Strengthened by this collective success, the collaboration takes on a new dimension today. Through this partnership, the Foundation and Etex affirm a shared ambition: to support innovative, responsible architecture oriented towards the living environments of the future. This partnership opens the way to new joint initiatives in service of young architects, creators and innovators from around the world.
