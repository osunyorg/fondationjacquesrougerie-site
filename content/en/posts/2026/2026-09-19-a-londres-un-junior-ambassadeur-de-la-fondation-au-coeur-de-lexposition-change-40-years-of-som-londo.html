In London, a Junior Ambassador of the Foundation at the heart of the exhibition "Change: 40 Years of SOM London," how does architecture reflect the world around us, and how does it help reinvent it?
This is the question at the heart of the exhibition "Change: 40 Years of SOM London," in which Thomas Goessler, Junior Ambassador of the Jacques Rougerie Foundation - Académie des beaux-arts, had the opportunity to take part from September 12 to 19, 2026, at Marylebone Hall.
Organized as part of the London Design Festival, the exhibition presented architecture and design as an ever-evolving cultural practice.
For one week, the public was able to discover four decades of projects and SOM's reflections on the future of design through four chapters: Our Practice, Design, Society, and Economy. Guided tours and meetings with young professionals punctuated the event.
For Thomas, this experience was an opportunity to get concretely involved in an international project.
It illustrates the Foundation's mission: to support a new generation of architects, designers, and innovators and to showcase their contribution to the architecture of tomorrow.

In London, a Junior Ambassador of the Foundation at the heart of the exhibition "Change: 40 Years of SOM London," how does architecture reflect the world around us, and how does it help reinvent it?

This is the question at the heart of the exhibition "Change: 40 Years of SOM London," in which Thomas Goessler, Junior Ambassador of the Jacques Rougerie Foundation - Académie des beaux-arts, had the opportunity to take part from September 12 to 19, 2026, at Marylebone Hall.

Organized as part of the London Design Festival, the exhibition presented architecture and design as an ever-evolving cultural practice.

For one week, the public was able to discover four decades of projects and SOM's reflections on the future of design through four chapters: Our Practice, Design, Society, and Economy. Guided tours and meetings with young professionals punctuated the event.

For Thomas, this experience was an opportunity to get concretely involved in an international project. It illustrates the Foundation's mission: to support a new generation of architects, designers, and innovators and to showcase their contribution to the architecture of tomorrow.
