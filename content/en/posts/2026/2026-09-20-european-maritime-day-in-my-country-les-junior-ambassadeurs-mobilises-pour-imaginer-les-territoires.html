On September 19 and 20, as part of the European initiative European Maritime Day in My Country, Teresa Rodríguez García, European Regional Coordinator of the Junior Ambassadors, organized, in collaboration with the Jacques Rougerie Foundation - Académie des beaux-arts, the international online seminar "Designing Coastal Futures."
This gathering brought together an international community of architects, designers, researchers, and innovators around the major issues related to the future of coastal and marine territories.
For two days, participants shared their reflections and experiences on different ways of rethinking our relationship with the sea and imagining new coastal futures: Coastal resilience, climate change adaptation, ocean sustainability, marine ecosystem regeneration, and the development of the blue economy were the main themes of the event, highlighting the need to combine scientific knowledge, creativity, and innovation to respond to the transformations of coastal and marine environments.
The program also gave an important place to art, design, and speculative futures, opening a space for reflection aimed at going beyond the responses given to current challenges. This approach made it possible to question the relationships between human beings, architecture, and the ocean, while exploring new ways of inhabiting, designing, and living with marine environments.
Bringing together: Ryan Wong, Inge de Beer, Handre de la Rey, Arol Jiokeng, Manon Drouillard, Jose Carlo Bolaños Padilla, Mohamed Emad, Noémie De Araujo Le Prince, Maram Abbas, Brayane Ndondji, and Joress Modjo, the seminar also illustrated the richness of the Foundation's international network of Junior Ambassadors. The diversity of backgrounds, disciplines, and territories represented made it possible to bring about new connections and encourage the sharing of knowledge around contemporary maritime issues.
Through this international mobilization, the Jacques Rougerie Foundation-Académie des beaux-arts continues its ambition to support a new generation of architects, designers, scientists, and innovators committed to the territories of tomorrow. By encouraging dialogue between disciplines and cultures, it helps bring forth new solutions for more resilient, regenerative, and sustainable coastlines.
This initiative fits fully within the Foundation's mission: to create bridges between architecture, science, technology, art and the environment to imagine new ways of inhabiting our planet. "Designing Coastal Futures" thus demonstrates the ability of the Junior Ambassadors to mobilize collectively and to carry, on an international scale, a forward-looking reflection on the future of our coasts and oceans.
