On the occasion of the first Pan-African Architecture Biennial, organized in Nairobi from September 6 to 11, 2026, Fondation Jacques Rougerie-Académie des beaux-arts brought together architects, urban planners and territorial stakeholders around the major challenges of African architecture tomorrow. Through an exhibition devoted to the projects of its African Junior Ambassadors and its participation in roundtable discussions, the Foundation highlighted a new generation committed to facing the challenges of urbanization, climate change and the transformation of living environments. This participation strengthens the Foundation's ties with the African continent and opens new perspectives for cooperation around architecture, innovation and the adaptation of territories.

body: >-


From September 6 to 11, 2026, Nairobi hosted the first Pan-African Architecture Biennial, bringing together architects, urban planners, experts and territorial stakeholders from 54 nations.

Alongside Etex, the Fondation Jacques Rougerie-Académie des beaux-arts participated in this inaugural edition as a partner and supporter, contributing to exchanges dedicated to the transformation of African territories.
Through an exhibition dedicated to the projects of its African Junior Ambassadors, the Foundation highlighted a new generation of committed architects whose projects offer responses to the continent's major challenges: urban growth, climate adaptation, rising sea levels and changing living environments.

For five days, round tables, meetings and exchanges made it possible to compare experiences and perspectives around a central question: how to design African housing and cities of tomorrow? The Foundation was represented by Amine Lehna, director of international relations, who participated in various exchanges and meetings with African and international stakeholders present in Nairobi.  

This first Pan-African Architecture Biennial thus opens new perspectives for cooperation for the Foundation, particularly around architecture, innovation, territorial adaptation and knowledge transfer. It also reflects the desire to strengthen the network of Junior Ambassadors and to support young generations imagining new responses to the transformations of their continent.

Through its participation in this inaugural edition, the

Fondation Jacques Rougerie-Académie des beaux-arts

strengthens its commitment in Africa and its ambition to create bridges between generations, territories and disciplines to contribute to the invention of living environments for tomorrow.
