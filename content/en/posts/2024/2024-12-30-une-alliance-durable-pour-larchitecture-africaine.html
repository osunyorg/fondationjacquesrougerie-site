🌍 A Partnership Between the Fondation Jacques Rougerie - Académie des beaux-arts, L'Ordre national des Architectes du Cameroun (ONAC) and ARCHITALK. The Fondation Jacques Rougerie - Académie des beaux-arts was honored to welcome Mr. Jean-Christophe Ndongo, President of the L'Ordre national des Architectes du Cameroun (ONAC) and Mr. Cédrix Tsambang, Director of the Junior Ambassadors program, to formalize an ambitious partnership. This collaboration places Africa at the heart of our shared ambition: to merge arts, sciences, and technology with nature 🌿, addressing the critical challenges of climate change and rising sea levels 🌊. 🌍 A Partnership Between the Fondation Jacques Rougerie - Académie des beaux-arts, L'Ordre national des Architectes du Cameroun (ONAC) and ARCHITALK.

The Fondation Jacques Rougerie - Académie des beaux-arts was honored to welcome Mr. Jean-Christophe Ndongo, President of the L'Ordre national des Architectes du Cameroun (ONAC) and Mr. Cédrix Tsambang, Director of the Junior Ambassadors program, to formalize an ambitious partnership.

This collaboration places Africa at the heart of our shared ambition: to merge arts, sciences, and technology with nature 🌿, addressing the critical challenges of climate change and rising sea levels 🌊. Together, we aim to design innovative and sustainable architectural solutions tailored to the unique needs of African coastal territories, fostering resilience and creativity.

Jacques ROUGERIE shared the Foundation’s mission to rekindle our connection with the environment ✨, emphasizing the fundamental role of architecture as a tool for transformation and hope.
